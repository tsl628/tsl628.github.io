Thomas Lu

Contact: tslu (at) umich (dot) edu

Hello! I’m a PhD student at the University of Michigan, advised by Gabriel Poesia. My research interests are broadly in the areas of AI for math, formal reasoning, and reinforcement learning. In summer 2026, I interned at Pramaana Labs, working on post-training for agentic formal reasoning.

I did my undergraduate work at UC Berkeley, where I was very fortunate to have been advised by Gireeja Ranade and Gasper Begus. At Berkeley, I worked on LeanTutor, a formally verified co-reasoning system that received a grant from the Renaissance Philanthropy AI for Math Fund. I was also the recipient of the 2026 EECS Departmental Citation.

At Berkeley, I was part of Combat Robotics at Berkeley, the team that designs and builds Glitch, a 250-pound battlebot. We competed in World Championships VI and VII on the BattleBots TV Show, and won Rookie of the Year on our debut in World Championship VI.
